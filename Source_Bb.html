Optimizes the modulation of the I/Q modulator for a subset of measurement requirement.

Parameters:

ipartq_gain – Dynamic range of 16 dB divided into 2 dB steps.

  • DB0 | DB2 | DB4 | DB6 | DB8: Activates the specified gain of 0 dB, +2 dB, +4 dB, +6 dB, +8 dB

  • DBM2 | DBM4: Activates the specified gain of -2 dB, -4 dB
