AvionicCarrFreqModeEnum Mode#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:[BB]:ILS:[GS]:FREQuency:MODE
AvionicCarrFreqModeEnum value = driver.Source.Bb.Ils.Gs.Frequency.Mode;
driver.Source.Bb.Ils.Gs.Frequency.Mode = AvionicCarrFreqModeEnum.DECimal;

Sets the mode for the carrier frequency of the signal.

mode: DECimal | USER | ICAO

  • DECimal | USER: Activates user-defined variation of the carrier frequency.

  • ICAO: Activates variation in predefined steps according to standard ILS transmitting frequencies (see Table “ILS ICAO channels and frequencies (MHz) “) .
