Defines the time delay of both I and Q vectors between the marker signal at the marker outputs relative to the signal generation start. A positive value means that the I and Q vectors delay relative to the marker/trigger and vice versa. Output / Min /s / Max /s / Increment

  • IQOutput<ch> / 500E-9 / 500E-9 / 1E-12

  • DIGital / 500E-9 / 500E-9 / 1E-12
