Sets the digital interface used to estimate the sample rate.

source: DIN | HSDin

  • DIN: Estimates the sample rate based on the digital input signal.

  • HSDin: Enabled for [:SOURcehw]:BBIN:DIGital:INTerfaceHSDin.
