HrpUwbMacLenAddressEnum LdAddress#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:BB:HUWB:MACHeader:LDADdress

driver.Source.Bb.Huwb.MacHeader.LdAddress = HrpUwbMacLenAddressEnum.L0;

Sets the length of the destination address field. You can set lengths of zero octets, two octets or eight octets.

lenDestAddr: L0 | L2 | L8

  • L0: Sets destination address length to zero octets.

  • L2: Sets destination address length to two octets.

  • L8: Sets destination address length to eight octets.
