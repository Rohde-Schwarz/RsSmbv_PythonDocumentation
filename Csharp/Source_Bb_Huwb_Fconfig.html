ZigBeePhrLengthInSymbolsEnum PhrLength#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:BB:HUWB:FCONfig:PHRLength
ZigBeePhrLengthInSymbolsEnum value = driver.Source.Bb.Huwb.Fconfig.PhrLength;

Queries the length of the PHY header. The length of the PHY header depends on the selected operating band and SFD.

phrLength: PHL_2 | PHL_7

  • PHL_2: For [:SOURcehw]:BB:HUWB:OBANd OB780 | OB868 | OB915 | OB2380 | OB2450.For [:SOURcehw]:BB:HUWB:OBANd OB5800 | OB6200 and [:SOURcehw]:BB:HUWB:SFD SFD_0.

  • PHL_7: For [:SOURcehw]:BB:HUWB:OBANd OB5800 | OB6200 and [:SOURcehw]:BB:HUWB:SFD SFD_1 | SFD_2 | SFD_3 | SFD_4.

HrpUwbSfdlEngthEnum SfdLength#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:BB:HUWB:FCONfig:SFDLength
HrpUwbSfdlEngthEnum value = driver.Source.Bb.Huwb.Fconfig.SfdLength;
driver.Source.Bb.Huwb.Fconfig.SfdLength = HrpUwbSfdlEngthEnum.SFDL_16;

Queries the symbol length of the start-of-frame delimiter (SFD) . The SFD length depends on the set SFD symbol sequence, see Table “SFD: indices and lengths”. In the mode [:SOURce<hw>]:BB:HUWB:STD OQPSK the SFD length is SFDL_2.

sfdLength: SFDL_8 | SFDL_64 | SFDL_2 | SFDL_4 | SFDL_16 | SFDL_32

Displays the symbol rate of the O-QPSK modulated signal. The symbol rate depends on the selected operating band and SFD.
