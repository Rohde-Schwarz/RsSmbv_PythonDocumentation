Selects the mode for the pulse modulation.

Parameters:

mode – SINGle | DOUBle | PTRain

  • SINGle: Generates a single pulse.

  • DOUBle: Generates two pulses within one pulse period.

  • PTRain: Generates a user-defined pulse train.Specify the pulse sequence with the commands:[:SOURcehw]:PULM:TRAin:ONTime[:SOURcehw]:PULM:TRAin:OFFTime[:SOURcehw]:PULM:TRAin:REPetition
