driver.source.bb.impairment.digital.set_delay(delay = 1.0)

Defines the time delay of both I and Q vectors between the marker signal at the marker outputs relative to the signal generation start. A positive value means that the I and Q vectors delay relative to the marker/trigger and vice versa.
